Village Block Blast

A relaxing block puzzle game with Bengali folk art themes.

Drag and drop colorful block shapes onto an 8x8 grid. Complete full rows or columns to clear them and score points. The look of the game draws on Bengali folk art such as nakshi kantha, rickshaw painting and pottery patterns.

  • Simple 8x8 block puzzle gameplay
  • Combos and score milestones
  • Built-in tutorial for new players
  • Free to play with ads
